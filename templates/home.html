How it works

  1. 1

    Type your readings

    pH, turbidity, TDS and (optional) dissolved oxygen from your test kit. One question per screen.

  2. 2

    See what is probably wrong

    AquaTrace checks your numbers against BIS and WHO limits and explains the finding in simple words.

  3. 3

    Report it to the right person

    High or critical results come with a ready-made message and a tap-to-call button for VCMC.
