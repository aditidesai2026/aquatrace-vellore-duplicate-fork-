How pH, TDS and turbidity relate

Low pH + high TDS

Acid water carrying a lot of dissolved salts. This often points to industrial or mine discharge, or to pipes corroding and releasing metals.

Normal pH + high TDS

Neutral salts — salinity. pH on its own would completely miss this, which is why AquaTrace always checks both numbers together.

Cloudy + low oxygen + smell

A strong sign of sewage getting into the supply. Do not drink it — report it to the City Health Officer immediately.

Turbidity also hides germs: particles protect bacteria and viruses from chlorine, so cloudy water is harder to disinfect. When pH is above 8.5, chlorine also works less well.
